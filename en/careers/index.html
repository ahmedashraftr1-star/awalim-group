    Careers·الوظائف

    Build the systems that run companies, with us

    We hire to the standard we ship to: balanced entries, tests that block regressions, and Arabic from the root. We ask less for years of experience than for something you built that we can read.

    Open roles (5) How we hire

    Why here·لماذا هنا

    Four things we guarantee you

    Real code reviewEverything you write is reviewed line by line — and everything we write is reviewed by you. That is how all of us learn.
    Fully remoteThe team has been distributed since day one. We measure output, not hours online, and we meet on a published schedule rather than by interruption.
    A product you watch runYou are not building a slide. You are building a system an accountant in Saudi Arabia or a family in Gaza will use tomorrow.
    A clear pathWritten expectations for every level, and a review every six months against criteria known in advance — no surprises.

    Senior backend engineer

    Enterprise systemsFull time Remote — Gulf time ±3 hours preferred4+ years

    You build the heart of Smart Accountant: the ledger engine, posting, and multi-tenant isolation on three layers. Precision here is not a feature — it is the condition.

    What you'll do

    • Designing data schemas and migrations that can be reviewed and reverted
    • Writing accounting logic where no unbalanced entry passes

    What we need

    • PostgreSQL in depth: transactions, isolation levels, indexing and query plans
    • TypeScript on the server with schema validation on every request
    • Automated tests you write before the feature, not after
    • The ability to explain an architectural decision in writing

    Counts in your favour

    An accounting background or ERP experienceDrizzle or any ORM that emits exported migrations

    How we hire·كيف نوظّف

    Four steps. No riddles.

    01

    Apply with a project

    Send something you built — a repository, a published app, or a design file — and two lines about a hard decision you made in it.

    02

    A 30-minute call

    We talk about that project specifically: why that decision, and what you would change about it today.

    03

    A paid practical task

    A task from our real work, with a sensible time limit, and paid. We do not ask for free labour.

    04

    A decision within a week

    We answer yes or no with a written reason. If it is no, we say precisely what was missing.

    Next step

    Ready? Send us something you built.

    We do not want a polished CV. Send something you built and two lines about a hard decision you made in it.
