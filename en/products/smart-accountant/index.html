    1. Scan. A paper invoice, a photo from a phone, or a PDF from email — the document box takes it as it is, with no re-entry.
    2. Extract. The system reads the vendor, the date, the net and the tax, and proposes the right account from your own chart of accounts.
    3. Balance. A double entry: debit equals credit — or it does not pass. No in-between state, no fixing it later.
    4. Post. On your approval the entry posts to the general ledger, and balances and reports update in the same moment.
    5. Close. IFRS financial statements ready for the auditor, and a monthly close after which nothing changes quietly.

    Features·المزايا

    What it actually does

    A full chart of accounts and journalA ledger per account, automatic posting, and upward balance aggregation with the correct sign.
    Operational modules, readySales, purchasing, inventory, fixed assets and depreciation, banks, and cost centres.
    HR and payroll on IAS 19Employees, attendance, leave, and a monthly payroll run that posts its entries and provisions automatically.
    The smart document boxPhotograph the invoice with your camera — the system extracts the data and proposes an entry that is balanced and accounting-correct.
    The financial decision roomA financial health indicator, early alerts, a cash-flow forecast, and «what if» simulation before you decide.
    Data isolation on three layersA route guard, a scoped storage layer, and row policies in the database — defence in depth.

    Architecture·البنية التقنية

    How it is built

    • TypeScript end to end — contracts shared between client and server
    • PostgreSQL with Drizzle ORM and exported, reviewable migrations
    • Schema validation on every request — no data enters unchecked
    • Comprehensive automated tests over the critical accounting paths
    • A natively right-to-left interface with tuned Arabic typography and tabular numerals
